ETF CATEGORIES

Every Type of ETF Market

Exchange Traded Funds combine the diversification of a mutual fund with the flexibility of share trading. Access every asset class through a single instrument with Veloci Global Markets.

Equity ETFs

Track broad market indices like the S&P 500, NASDAQ and global market indices. Equity ETFs offer efficient exposure to hundreds of companies with minimal cost and maximum flexibility.

Commodity ETFs

Gain diversified exposure to gold, silver, oil and other commodities through ETFs. Commodity ETFs track the price of physical assets without the need for storage or ownership.

Bond ETFs

Access government and corporate bond markets through ETFs. Bond ETFs provide exposure to fixed income instruments with the liquidity benefits of equity trading.

Sector ETFs

Focus on specific market sectors including technology, healthcare, energy and financial services. Sector ETFs allow you to capitalise on trends in specific areas of the economy.
