Frequently Asked Questions
What is copy trading?
Copy trading is a form of social investing where you automatically mirror the trades of experienced traders. When the master trader opens or closes a position, the same action is executed proportionally in your account. You benefit from their expertise without needing to manage trades manually.
How does the fee structure work?
Each master trader sets their own performance fee, typically between 15% and 30% of profits generated for followers. There are no subscription fees or monthly charges — fees are only deducted when you make a profit. A small platform fee (2% of AUM annually) also applies, which covers infrastructure and compliance costs.
Can I stop copying at any time?
Yes, absolutely. You can stop copying a trader at any time with no penalties or lock-up periods. Once you stop, all mirrored positions will be closed at current market prices and your funds will be returned to your available balance. You can then choose a different trader or manage your account independently.
Is my capital protected?
While we rigorously vet all master traders and require a minimum track record, copy trading does not guarantee capital protection. Trading involves risk and you may lose part or all of your investment. We recommend diversifying across multiple traders and only investing capital you can afford to lose. Risk controls such as stop-loss limits per trader allocation are available in your settings.
