1
Dashboard
Your first stop — a snapshot of the entire platform
Sidebar → Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see when you log in. It gives you a summary of everything important happening on the platform right now.

What You SeeWhat It Means
Total UsersTotal number of registered accounts on the platform.
Total DepositsCombined value of all approved deposits ever made.
Pending WithdrawalsWithdrawal requests waiting for your approval.
Active PositionsNumber of trades currently open across all users.
Recent Sign-upsThe newest users who just registered — shown with name and email.
1
Check pending items first.

Look at the orange-highlighted stat cards to see if there are pending withdrawals or KYC submissions that need your attention today.

2
Add a new user quickly.

Click the + Add User button on the Dashboard to create a user account manually without them having to register themselves.

3
Click "View All" to drill down.

The recent sign-ups panel has a View All button that takes you directly to User Management with the full list.

2
Live Activity
Watch what's happening on the platform in real time
Sidebar → Live Activity

Live Activity is a running log of everything happening on the platform — logins, deposits, trades, withdrawals — as it happens. You do not need to refresh the page; events appear automatically.

Best use: Keep this page open in a second browser tab during busy trading hours so you can see activity without leaving the section you're working in.

Note: Activity events are not stored permanently — they only show events since the page was loaded. Refresh to clear the log and start fresh.

3
User Management
Your full control over every user account
Sidebar → User Management

This is where you manage every registered user. You can view, search, filter, edit, and take actions like suspending or banning accounts.

1
Find a specific user.

Type their name or email into the search bar at the top. Results filter as you type — no need to press Enter.

2
Filter by status.

Use the tab buttons — All Active Suspended Banned — to narrow the list to a specific group of users.

3
Click a user row to open their profile.

A side panel opens showing their full details — name, email, registration date, KYC status, account plan, and balance.

4
Take action on a user.

From the user profile panel you can: edit their details, change their account plan (Starter → Silver → Gold etc.), suspend them temporarily, or ban them permanently.

5
Add a new user manually.

Click the + Add User button in the top right. Fill in their name, email, and password. This creates a full account for them without them having to register.

ActionWhat It Does
SuspendTemporarily locks the user out. They cannot log in. You can undo this anytime.
BanPermanently blocks the account. The user sees a deactivation message at login.
Change PlanUpgrades or downgrades the user's tier (Starter / Silver / Gold / Platinum / Elite).
Edit DetailsUpdate their first name, last name, email, or phone number.

Tip: You can also search users using the search bar in the top navigation bar — it works from any page in the admin panel.

4
KYC / Verification Queue
Review and approve user identity submissions
Sidebar → KYC / Verification

When a user submits their identity documents for KYC verification, their submission appears here. You review the documents and either approve or reject them.

1
Open a pending submission.

Submissions with status Pending need your review. Click the row to open it.

2
Review the documents.

You will see the user's name, date of birth, document type (e.g. Passport), country of issue, a photo of the document, and their selfie. Check they match.

3
Approve or Reject.

Click Approve to verify the user — their KYC status turns Verified and their withdrawal limits increase. Click Reject if the documents are invalid — the user is notified to resubmit.

Important: When you approve or reject a KYC submission, an automated notification email is sent to the admin help email so you always have a record.

5
Trading Accounts
Full overview of every user's account — plan, balance, equity, and KYC status
Sidebar → Trading Accounts

This section gives you a complete account-level view of every registered user on the platform — their plan tier, current balance, equity, account status, and KYC verification state, all in one table.

ColumnWhat It Shows
UserThe account holder's email and full name.
PlanTheir current tier — Starter Silver Gold Platinum Elite — shown as a colour-coded badge.
BalanceTheir available cash balance.
EquityBalance adjusted for any open trade positions.
StatusActive or Suspended — whether the account is currently usable.
KYCApproved / Pending / Unverified — identity verification status.
JoinedThe date the user registered on the platform.
AdjustOpens the balance adjustment modal for that user directly from this page.
1
Spot unverified users quickly.

The KYC column makes it easy to identify users who haven't completed identity verification — follow up with them or flag their account.

2
Adjust a balance directly.

Click Adjust on any row to credit or debit that user's balance without having to navigate to Balances & P&L.

3
Refresh anytime.

Click the refresh button (↻) in the top right of the page to reload the latest data from the database.

This page loads automatically when you click Trading Accounts in the sidebar. Data comes directly from user profiles — one row per registered user.

6
Balances & P&L
View and manually adjust user account balances
Sidebar → Balances & P&L

This is where you go when you need to manually credit or deduct from a user's balance — for example, after a deposit is confirmed or to apply a bonus.

1
Find the user.

Search by name or email using the search or filter controls at the top of the table.

2
Click on their row.

This opens the balance adjustment panel for that specific user.

3
Enter the amount and save.

Enter the amount to credit (positive) or deduct (negative). Add a note for your own records, then confirm. The change is instant.

Be careful: Balance adjustments are immediate and logged in the Audit Log. Always double-check the amount before confirming.

7
Open Positions
Monitor and manage all live user trades
Sidebar → Open Positions

This section shows every trade that is currently open across all users. You can see the instrument, direction, size, entry price, current P&L, and close the position if needed.

ColumnWhat It Means
UserWho opened the trade.
InstrumentWhat they are trading (e.g. BTC/USD, EUR/USD).
DirectionBUY means they are betting the price goes up. SELL means they are betting it goes down.
Size / LotsHow large the trade is.
Entry PriceThe price when they opened the trade.
Current P&LHow much profit or loss the trade is showing right now.
1
To close a position manually:

Click on the position row and use the Close Position button. This ends the trade at the current market price and settles the P&L to the user's balance.

2
To set a profit target:

Click on a position and enter a profit target. When the trade's P&L reaches that level, the system can automatically close it.

8
Profit Targets
Define and manage trade profit target rules
Sidebar → Profit Targets

Profit Targets let you pre-set rules for when a trade should be closed based on reaching a specific profit level. This is useful for managing risk across user accounts.

1
Select a user and their open trade.

Use the dropdowns to pick the user and which of their positions you want to set a target on.

2
Enter the target profit amount.

Type the dollar amount at which the trade should be closed (e.g. $500). When the position's P&L reaches this, it closes automatically.

3
Save the target.

Click Save. The target is now active and will trigger automatically.

9
Deposits
Review and approve incoming client deposits
Sidebar → Deposits

When a user submits a deposit (crypto), it lands here with Pending status. You verify the payment and then approve it to credit their account.

1
Check the pending deposits tab.

Filter to Pending to see deposits waiting for approval. You will see the user's name, amount, coin, and network.

2
Verify the transaction externally.

Check your crypto wallet or the relevant blockchain explorer to confirm the payment has actually arrived before approving.

3
Click Approve.

This instantly credits the deposit amount to the user's balance and sends them an automated confirmation email saying their deposit was approved.

4
Or click Reject.

If the payment cannot be verified, reject it. The user is notified and no balance change occurs.

Tip: The deposit email the user receives is fully branded with the Veloci logo and template — no extra action needed from you.

10
Withdrawals
Process and approve client withdrawal requests
Sidebar → Withdrawals

When a user requests a withdrawal, it appears here. You review the request, send the funds externally, then approve it in the panel to mark it as completed.

1
Open a pending withdrawal.

Click on a Pending withdrawal to see the user's name, amount, destination wallet address, coin, and network.

2
Send the funds.

Go to your exchange or wallet, send the exact amount to the destination address they provided. Make sure the network matches (e.g. TRC-20, ERC-20).

3
Click Approve in the panel.

Once the transfer is sent, click Approve. This marks the withdrawal as complete and sends the user a confirmation email.

Always send the funds before clicking Approve. Approving deducts the balance from the user's account. If you approve before sending, their balance goes down but they never receive the money.

You can temporarily disable all withdrawals from System Settings → Platform Toggles if you need to pause them for maintenance or review.

11
All Transactions
The complete financial ledger of the platform
Sidebar → All Transactions

This section shows every financial transaction ever recorded on the platform — deposits, withdrawals, balance adjustments, and fees — in one searchable list.

1
Filter by type or user.

Use the filter dropdowns to show only deposits, only withdrawals, or transactions for a specific user.

2
Export to CSV.

Click the Export CSV button to download the full transaction list as a spreadsheet — useful for accounting or compliance.

12
Announcements
Push notification messages directly to user dashboards
Sidebar → Announcements

Announcements let you push a message that appears in the notification bell on every user's dashboard. Use this for platform updates, scheduled maintenance, promotions, or important alerts.

1
Write your announcement.

Enter a short title (e.g. "Scheduled Maintenance") and a message body explaining what users need to know.

2
Choose the type.

Select a type — Info, Success, Warning, or Alert — this controls the colour of the notification badge users see.

3
Send.

Click Send Announcement. It immediately appears in the notification bell for all users on the platform.

4
Delete old announcements.

Old announcements remain until you delete them. Clean them up regularly so users only see relevant notifications.

Best for: Maintenance windows, new feature launches, market closure notices, or urgent account-wide alerts.

13
Markets Control
Enable or disable trading instruments and manage spreads
Sidebar → Markets Control

Markets Control gives you direct control over which trading instruments are available to users — stocks, crypto pairs, forex, commodities — and lets you adjust the spread (buy/sell difference) for any instrument.

1
Enable or disable an instrument.

Find the instrument in the list (you can search by name). Toggle it off to hide it from users — they will no longer see it on the trading pages.

2
Adjust the spread.

Click on an instrument to edit its spread. The spread is the difference between the buy and sell price — a higher spread means more margin for the platform.

3
Save your changes.

Changes take effect immediately for all users — no page refresh needed on their end.

Disabling an instrument does not close existing positions in that instrument — it only prevents new trades from being opened.

14
Email Users
Send branded emails and manage billing format templates
Sidebar → Email Users

This section has two parts: the Compose panel for sending emails right now, and the Billing Formats library for storing and reusing email templates.

Part A — Sending an Email
1
Choose who to send to.

Select Specific User to email one person, or All Users to broadcast to everyone on the platform at once.

2
Write the subject and body.

Use formatting codes to style your message: wrap text in *asterisks* to make it orange and highlighted. Wrap in #hashes# to make it bold.

3
Click Send Email.

The email is sent instantly using the branded Veloci template — complete with the logo, your message, and the support contact in the footer.

4
Check Sent History.

The right panel shows all emails ever sent — recipient, subject, and timestamp. Click View to read the full email again.

Part B — Billing Formats for Clients

Billing Formats is a private library where you save reusable email templates — upgrade fee requests, billing reminders, account warnings, deposit scripts. Write it once, reuse it forever. Formats sync across all your devices (phone and desktop) because they are stored in the cloud, not the browser.

1
Create a new format.

Click + New Format. Fill in the Template Name, Email Subject, Email Body, and optionally a Manager Explanation. Click Save Format.

2
Copy Subject or Body to clipboard.

Find the format in the list and click Subject or Body to copy that part instantly — paste it into WhatsApp, Gmail, or anywhere else.

3
Load directly into Compose.

Click Use in Compose (or Use on mobile) to automatically fill the compose form above with the subject and body — then just pick the recipient and send.

4
View your Manager Explanation.

Each format can have a private Manager Explanation — your internal notes or talking points for that scenario. Click the purple Manager Explanation button on a format to read it. It is never visible to users.

5
Reorder formats.

Use the ↑ ↓ arrow buttons on each format to move it up or down in the list. You can also drag and drop by holding the grip handle (⠿) on the left of each row.

6
Edit or delete.

Click Edit to update a format's name, subject, body, or explanation. Click Delete to permanently remove it.

Part C — Sent History

Every email sent through the compose form is saved to Sent History. Click View on any row to re-read the exact email that was sent. On mobile, only the 3 most recent emails are shown by default — tap View All History to see the full list.

Tip: Use the Manager Explanation field to write the reasoning behind each billing format — e.g. "Use this when the account has been suspended for 3+ days and the user still hasn't deposited". That context is only visible to you.

15
System Settings
Platform-wide configuration and global toggles
Sidebar → System Settings

System Settings controls the overall behaviour of the platform. Changes here affect every user immediately.

SettingWhat It Does
Platform NameThe name shown across the platform and in emails.
Support EmailThe email address shown to users when they need help.
Registrations OpenToggle off to stop new users from signing up.
Live Trading EnabledToggle off to disable all trading for all users at once.
Deposits EnabledToggle off to stop the deposit flow for all users.
Withdrawals EnabledToggle off to freeze all withdrawals. You can restrict this to all users or specific users only.
Crypto WalletsSet the deposit wallet addresses for each cryptocurrency (BTC, ETH, USDT, etc.) that users will send funds to.

Always click Save after making any change in System Settings. Changes are not applied until saved.

Tip: When updating crypto wallet addresses, double-check the address character by character. A wrong deposit address means lost funds.

16
Audit Log
A permanent record of every admin action taken
Sidebar → Audit Log

The Audit Log records every action performed in the admin panel — who did what, to which user, at what time, from what IP address. It cannot be edited or deleted.

ColumnWhat It Shows
TimeExact date and time the action was taken.
AdminWhich admin account performed the action.
ActionWhat was done (e.g. deposit_approved, user_suspended, balance_adjusted).
TargetWhich user was affected.
DetailsExtra information about the action (e.g. amount, old value vs new value).
IP AddressThe IP address of the admin who performed the action.
1
Filter by action type or date.

Use the filter controls to narrow down to a specific type of action or a date range.

2
Export for compliance.

Click Export CSV to download the full audit log as a spreadsheet. Useful for compliance checks or internal reviews.

The Audit Log is your safety net. If anything unexpected happens on the platform — an incorrect balance change, an accidental approval — check here first to see exactly what happened and when.

17
Users Log
Complete login activity history across all user accounts
Sidebar → System → Users Log

The Users Log shows the login history of every user on the platform — not just your own. Each row is a single login event, showing exactly when it happened, where it came from, and what device was used. This is the admin-side equivalent of the security log users see in their own account settings.

ColumnWhat It Shows
UserThe email and name of the account that logged in.
TimeExact date and time of the login event.
LocationCity and country detected from the login IP (e.g. "Lagos, NG").
IP AddressThe IP address the login came from.
DeviceMobile, Tablet, or Desktop.
BrowserWhich browser was used — Chrome, Safari, Firefox, etc.
OSThe operating system — Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, etc.
1
Find a specific user's activity.

Use the search box at the top right to filter by email or name. Only that user's login events will show instantly — no page reload needed.

2
Investigate suspicious logins.

If a user reports their account was accessed without permission, check here for logins from unfamiliar locations, IP addresses, or devices.

3
Refresh the list.

Click the ↻ button to reload the latest 500 login events from the database.

Tip: If you see a user logging in from multiple countries within a short time, it may indicate a compromised account. Suspend the account immediately from User Management and contact the user.

The Users Log shows the last 500 login events across all accounts, ordered from most recent to oldest. This page is only visible to admins — users cannot see each other's login history.
