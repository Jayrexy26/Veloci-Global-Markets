Frequently Asked Questions
How do I upgrade my plan?
To upgrade your account plan, click the Upgrade button on any higher-tier plan card. This will open a contact dialog where you can reach our support team via email, live chat, or Telegram. Our team will guide you through the upgrade process, which typically involves verifying your account balance and completing any required KYC steps.
How long does the upgrade take?
Once you contact our support team and meet the plan requirements (minimum balance, KYC verification), upgrades are typically processed within 24â€“48 hours on business days. Premium and Elite tier upgrades may take slightly longer due to additional verification requirements. You will receive a notification once your plan has been updated.
Can I downgrade my plan?
Yes, plan downgrades are possible by contacting our support team. Please note that downgrading will result in reduced trading limits, higher fees, and loss of premium features associated with your current plan. Your existing trades and funds are not affected by a plan change. Contact support@velociglobal.pro to request a downgrade.
What happens to my funds if I upgrade?
Upgrading your plan has no impact on your funds. Your balance, open positions, and trade history remain completely unchanged. The upgrade only affects your fee tier, withdrawal limits, leverage, and access to premium features. All changes take effect from the moment the upgrade is processed.
